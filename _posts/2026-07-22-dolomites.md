## Alta Via

### Day 1

Starting point after 5 hours of bus!

View of Lago di Briaes, traditional starting 
point of Alta Via 1.

In disbelief that it starts off this good.

Dusk vibes from the first rifugio. 
Showers were not working but there were 
plenty of full water bottles in the shower 
to use.

### Day 2

A short day with very steep downhill.
First of many mountainous coffee stops of 
the trip.

Pony and cows!


### Day 3

By far our longest day. About 16 miles, 
so many passes…

Loved how every corner seemed to hide a 
new pass or meadow or pasture unlike any 
we’d seen before.


We don’t quite believe we’re gonna go down 
from this pass


Two photos looking back at the pass we 
descended from.




### Day 4

After such a hard day yesterday, we took 
today easy. Instead of the 9 mile hike into 
cortina, we took the cable car up to 
Rifugio Lagazuoi so we could explore the 
WWI tunnels. Yes we did rent helmets and 
did bonk our heads on the top of the tunnels 
many times.

Got some really good spinach dumplings at 
the rifugio. Also espresso. The views were 
incredible but I guess I didn’t take a photo.

The hotel was nice, though I was briefly 
convinced that I had lost my wallet on the 
bus. Spent all of dinner pausing my bank 
cards and figuring out where to go for bus 
lost and found. Got back to the hotel and 
found my wallet under my laundry, which I 
had placed on my bedside table.


WWI trench with the best view?


### Day 5

The day did start with a bus from Cortina 
and a chairlift up to Cinque Torri. This 
is how they do hiking in Italy, and we’re 
into it.

Another day meandering up and down among 
towering peaks through meadow after meadow. 
Some cows along the way.

Cinque Torri!!


Cows!!

We started the day riiiiiight there!

This rifugio had weird vibes, but the 
cutest doggos

### Day 6

I feel like by this day we were in a good 
rhythm. Tried to stay on brand and be the
 last people to leave because we so 
meticulously packed for the day.

Started with some easy down and up the 
steeeep up. Saw our first rifugio “served 
by cable” where there’s a cable car just 
for supplies.

The sinks were really big at the rifugio so 
everyone did laundry, but as clothes were 
drying an evening thunderstorm came through. 
Pretty much everyone had clothes hanging off 
their backpacks the next day.


Flowers were popping of the whole way. 
This was the first time I thought to 
photograph it :3

This was the first day we had a good amount 
of time to play guitar. Our friend Carlos 
sang some 70s hits with us. A lady requested 
John Denver so we played Country Roads. 
We were told later that everyone who could 
hear us sang along.

Reserved for doggo


We ordered a Skiwasser and Bombardino from 
the menu since we didn’t know what they were. 
Our server told us it was a weird combo but 
we stayed the course. They did indeed not 
go together. Skiwasser was a raspberry 
flavored water. Bombardino was an egg nog, 
though very different than I’m used to. 
Bombardino apparently means “little bomb”.
