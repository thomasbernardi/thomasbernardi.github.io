### The Route
This portion of the trip had been vaguely planned
for months from back when Sam was thinking of
spending an extended period bikepacking. Visiting
Lars and biking through Norway seemed like a great
way to start. We looked around online and found a 
few different routes and narrowed down to one
that was the right distance for a two week trip.

At first we had been hoping to travel hut to hut,
but as we looked further into it we realized that
the hut infrastructure waa designed around hiking
trails rather than biking trails. So we planned
to bring tents which made the itinerary possible
and more flexible.

The route was originally supposed to start in
Vinstra but the train was booked so we started
in Lillehammer instead. The route would travel
south and west, meandering through the mountains
until reaching Flåm--a fjord town.

![Route Map](/assets/milk-route/map.jpeg)

### Day 1

The day started with some final adjustments to
our bike setups. I certainly took a lot of time
figuring out how I wanted to attach all my bags.
When we were still fiddling with our bikes at
9:10, I had doubts we would make the 9:34 train. 
But we managed to dash out the door, speed
through town and quickly walk our bikes to our
platform. We arrived just as the train pulled in.

During the ride Sam was glued to the window--the
views as we passed through the countryside were
stunning. Lars and I caught up on our reading.

![Train Ride](/assets/milk-route/photo-1.jpeg)

Once we got to Lillehammer, we stopped at a
sports store to get some last supplies,
caffeinated at a coffee shop, loaded up on
groceries and were off!!

![Lillehammer Bridge](/assets/milk-route/photo-2.jpeg)

The majority of the route was a long and gradual
climb through a beautiful agricultural valley.
Endless hay fields and pasture dotted with farm
houses seemed to go on forever.

![Valley](/assets/milk-route/photo-3.jpeg)

Lars waits for Tommy to catch up...
![Tommy Climbs](/assets/milk-route/photo-4.jpeg)

As the day wore on the clouds rolled in and the
air grew chilly. We entered some woods and had
more of a climb to go. Along the way we passed
some cute sheep...

![Sheep](/assets/milk-route/photo-5.jpeg)


Shortly before looking for a
place to camp we stopped to grab some more food.
We found we had voracious apetites and hoped for
hot food. There was not any so we gorged on
snacks.

We had one last small climb to the zone where we
thought to camp that night after which we scouted
around for a site. We found one close to a stream
and set up camp.
