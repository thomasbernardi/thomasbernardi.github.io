Between our bikepacking and backpacking trip we
decided to head down to Copenhagen to visit a
friend who was passing through. The train and bus
options were not looking great so we decided to
take a boat.

The boat left in the evening which meant we got
to sail down the Oslo fjord during the evening
light and even caught the sunset!

![Departing Oslo](/assets/copenhagen/photo-1.jpeg)

![Oslo Fjord](/assets/copenhagen/photo-2.jpeg)

![Sunset](/assets/copenhagen/photo-3.jpeg)

We slept very well--the boat rocking us to sleep.
In the morning we missed the sunrise but caught
some very nice morning sun.

![Morning Sun](/assets/copenhagen/photo-4.jpeg)

Sam decided to be silly when he was drinking his
morning coffee.

![Sam's Coffee](/assets/copenhagen/photo-5.jpeg)

We didn't see to many sights in Copenhagen, we
mostly took it easy. I did visit Christiania as
well as the Christiansborg Palace where the
Danish government is located, in addition to a
few horses.

![Christiania](/assets/copenhagen/photo-6.jpeg)

![Christiansborg](/assets/copenhagen/photo-8.jpeg)

On our last day we went kayaking! The big bucket
was to pick up trash but the bay was so clean we
only found one small piece.

![Kayaking](/assets/copenhagen/photo-9.jpeg)

The return trip to Oslo was also beautiful with
a sunset and a morning in the Oslo fjord.
