# Dolomites Part 2
## Tommy and Emmaline in Italy

The second part of my Italian adventure was to
meet up with my friend Emmaline while we were
both in the Alps! We met up in Bolzano and
found a cute hotel/hostel called Schwarze
Katz which was right on the bus lines to all
the hikes we were thinking of doing. The staff
wete a quaint combination of kind and helpful
but not super warm or enthusiastic. I got the
impression that they had been running this
place for at least a decade or two...

### Hike 1: Seiser Alm Bahn

After so many days of lots of elevation, I was
hoping to find a hike with more rolling
hills. Fortunately Emmaline had done a lot of
research and had just the hike in mind. After
a few hiccups with public transit we settled on
taking the expensive gondola up to an area
of meadow/farmland called Seiser Alm Bahn.

We made a lovely loop through the various
farms, up and down through the rolling pastures.
My friend and I spend the hike catching up and
taking in the views. The contrast of rolling fields
in the foreground and jagged rocks in the background
was absolutely stunning.

### Hike 2: Seceda

This day I was up for a bit more elevation
after an easier day. Just as we started the
hike we met some folks from Seattle who had moved
to Europe and had a summer apartment in the area.
They kindly gave us some recommendations about
our route (since there were many options) and we
went on our way.

The hike started with a big climb through the woods
and soon we emerged into alpine meadow. We were grateful
for the cool air and shade as our legs burned from
the ascent. After a morning of relatively peaceful
hiking we joined the more popular zone of the valley.

There were several peaks and rock formations that
people had come to see and photograph. There was
even one path to a popular photo spot that cost
a few euros to enter (!!!). We shared a moment of
disbelief with a fellow hiker before skipping that
trail.

The path in the upper left of this photo was the
one you had to pay for...
![Santa Cristina Gherdeina](/assets/dolomites-2/photo-4.jpeg)

The rest of the hike was a meandering descent
through a stunning valley full of farms and huts.
We took our time enjoying the different
perspectives on the peaks towering above us and
the valley spreading out below us.

We spotted, atop this impressive rock, a group
of people getting their photo taken from below. 
Too wholesome...

![Big Rock](/assets/dolomites-2/photo-5.jpeg)

And of course cows, living the life...

![Good Cows](/assets/dolomites-2/photo-6.jpeg)

Finally, as a reward for our hard work, we stopped
to get cake--our Seattle friends from earlier
had raved about these cakes. They did not
disappoint.
